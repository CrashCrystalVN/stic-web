About STIC Club

A Real-World STEM Playground for Da Nang Students

STIC (Science, Technology & Innovation Club) is a hands-on research, innovation, and makerspace built for passionate high school students in Da Nang City.

Our Mission

Building an open engineering community where every high schooler can access advanced tech, experiment with bold scientific ideas, and master real-world skills from day one.

Philosophy "Learn By Doing"

Beyond school theory, at STIC you build real prototypes: solder circuits, assemble robots, ship software, produce media, and lead engaging STEM experiments.

Core Values

What Guides STIC

Breakthrough Innovation

Core Value

Encouraging every idea — no matter how small. Transforming theoretical concepts into working prototypes from day one.

Learn by Doing

Core Value

No dogma, no passive reading. Real engineering skills come from hands-on work: soldering, coding, designing, and presenting.

Together We Build

Core Value

STIC is a family. Cross-functional teams support each other, senior mentors guide juniors, and we build products greater than any individual.

Real-World Impact

Core Value

Every project at STIC targets practical challenges for students, schools, and the Da Nang community — never built just for display.
